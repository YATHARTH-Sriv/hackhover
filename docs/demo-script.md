# Two-visit demo checklist

Use your chosen real place. Keep its exact location private. Record the two outings on separate visits and use your own observations; do not stage a result as a field finding.

## Capture

1. Show the home page and name the place. Show `gemma3:4b` ready in local setup.
2. Prepare visit one. Record the generated card and a short clip of ElevenLabs speech playback. Note approximate time spent in the app.
3. Leave the laptop and phone behind. Capture a short outdoor clip separately if practical. Note approximate minutes outside.
4. Back at the laptop, record a reflection. Show ElevenLabs transcription, correct any mistakes, then save. Keep the exact confirmed text for the write-up.
5. On a separate outing, prepare visit two. Expand **What this visit remembered** so the retrieved Backboard memory and its effect on the new Gemma card are visible together.
6. After the second outing, record and save a second reflection. Show both dated notes and the comparison in the notebook.

## Evidence to retain

- A short screen recording of both cards and speech playback.
- The exact first note, retrieved memory, and second card in one sequence.
- A short screen recording of audio transcription and the editable transcript.
- Honest in-app time and outside time estimates for both visits.
- A screenshot or clip of the local Ollama model running on the Mac.
- A short statement about one thing that failed or changed during the real test.

## Before publishing

- Hide API keys, account balances, personal paths, browser tabs, and the precise location.
- Confirm the repository excludes `.env.local`, `data/`, generated audio, and personal field notes.
- Check the current challenge rules and official post template. Include the required tags, repository, demo, open-weight model explanation, and only prize categories proven by the working demo.
